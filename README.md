# Indonesia to Germany Bridge

My girlfriend is from indonesia, bali and got here to germany an is offering now german a1 classes especially for indonesian people that live currently still in indonesia but want to move to germany. Build a website for that that supports / visulaizes the balinese / indonesian and german culture mix and shows what she offers and also give a possibility to contact her via a contact form
